# Governance Source Summaries

## EU AI Act Regulation (EU) 2024/1689

Useful structural lessons:

- lifetime event logging for high-risk AI systems;
- logs should support traceability, monitoring, and interpretation;
- human oversight should be proportional to risk, autonomy, and context of use;
- oversight actors need competence, training, authority, and the ability to interpret outputs and intervene.

CairnLab mapping:

- append-only transition ledger;
- DecisionTracePackage;
- HumanGate with competence/authority/liability scope;
- RiskAssessment controls.

## NIST AI RMF

Useful structural lessons:

- risk management should cover design, development, use, and evaluation;
- GOVERN is a cross-cutting function;
- trustworthy AI requires lifecycle test/evaluation/verification/validation.

CairnLab mapping:

- LifecycleContext;
- AssessmentRecord;
- GovernancePolicy;
- verifier certificates.

## ISO/IEC 42001

Useful structural lessons:

- AI management system approach;
- roles and responsibilities;
- documented information;
- risk and opportunity management;
- monitoring and continual improvement.

CairnLab mapping:

- ResponsibilityAssignment;
- risk-tiered controls;
- machine-readable registry and decision trace exports.
